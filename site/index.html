Anatomy of a cup

Every surface tells you how it was made.

  • 22k gold lustre rim

    A hair under 3 mm of liquid gold, brushed by hand and fired on at 780 °C. It softens the edge against your lip and warms with every refill.

  • Dip-and-drip glaze

    Cream over terracotta, laid down in one motion. The speckled iron flecks come from the clay itself rising through the glaze in the kiln.

  • Pulled handle

    Sized for two fingers and a thumb. Pulled from a single coil, tapered at both ends and joined while the clay is still alive.

  • Raw stoneware foot

    We leave the foot ring bare so you can see — and feel — what the cup is made from. Sanded smooth, so it never scratches a table.
